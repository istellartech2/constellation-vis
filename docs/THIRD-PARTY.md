# Third-party material

Project-authored material is Apache-2.0; third-party material keeps its own terms.

- The ground-station map uses [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) data (ODbL) and displays attribution. Its [tile policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/) requires a Referer, caching and no bulk prefetching. The map sends only the HTTPS origin via `strict-origin`; availability is best-effort.
- NASA and Natural Earth imagery, credits, terms and regeneration instructions are in [Earth textures](../public/assets/earth/README.md). The favicon, satellite marker and sample KML routes are project-authored. The sample ISS and NOAA 15 TLEs in `public/satellites.toml` were obtained from [CelesTrak](https://celestrak.org/); the project owner has confirmed their use. Follow its [usage policy](https://celestrak.org/usage-policy.php) and check the terms for other imported orbital data.
- Web and CLI builds generate `third-party-licenses.txt` with bundled dependencies' licenses and notices. `react-leaflet` and `@react-leaflet/core` use [Hippocratic License 2.1](https://github.com/PaulLeCam/react-leaflet/blob/master/LICENSE.md), including its additional use conditions. `size-sensor` has no published copyright notice or license text; the web build uses the project-authored `ResizeObserver` adapter instead and does not bundle it.
- The ITU filing catalog contains official links and reviewed interpretation notes, not copies of ITU databases. Users obtain SNS MDB files from ITU and open them locally; the application does not upload or proxy those files. Public access does not establish redistribution permission; consult the [ITU terms](https://www.itu.int/en/about/Pages/terms-of-use.aspx) and applicable service terms. See [filing import](filings-import.md) for source handling and conceptual-position assumptions.
- The CLI requires a separately installed Bun ≥ 1.3.11. If Bun is redistributed or embedded, check [Bun's license](https://bun.com/docs/project/license) and the linked libraries' obligations, including LGPL.

The license text overrides in `scripts/license-overrides/` are from [Radix](https://github.com/radix-ui/primitives/blob/main/LICENSE), [react-remove-scroll-bar](https://github.com/theKashey/react-remove-scroll-bar/blob/7301c160fda44cb8cf2b9fdfde61efad35736196/LICENSE), and the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/). The KaTeX font notices come from the installed font sources. The react-remove-scroll-bar 2.3.8 npm package declares MIT but omits its license file; its override reproduces the upstream MIT text and copyright notice verbatim. The project owner confirmed that this notice matches the historical notice. Review overrides when dependencies change.

The `@nodable/entities` 3.1.0 npm package omits its license file. Its override is the upstream [Nodable MIT notice](https://github.com/nodable/val-parsers/blob/4fe544c219e52cb6e302c43a88824a8333babe3e/LICENSE). The `isarray` 1.0.0 MIT notice is included from its published README. Both notices are incorporated in the generated third-party license output, including when these dependencies are bundled in the MDB worker.
